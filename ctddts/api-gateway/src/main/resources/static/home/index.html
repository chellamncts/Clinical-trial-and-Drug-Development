How a trial moves through the system

Five connected stages, from study setup to lab tracking.

1

Protocol & Sites

Admins design protocols and activate clinical sites for enrollment.

2

Enrollment

Investigators screen subjects, capture consent and randomize arms.

3

Visits & CRF

Data managers schedule visits, record CRFs and lock completed forms.

4

Safety

Pharmacovigilance reports adverse events and escalates SAEs.

5

Lab & IP

Coordinators track samples and dispense investigational product.
